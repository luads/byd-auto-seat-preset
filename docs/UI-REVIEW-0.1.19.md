# Seat and mirror trial review

| Before | After | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Seat-only axis selector and fixed pulse length. | One axis selector adds four mirror axes; seat duration offers 100/200/300 ms. | Keep optional experiments together. |
| Generic direction text for every operation. | Mirrors show Left/Right or Up/Down; mirror duration is locked to 100 ms. | Make the chosen physical trial clear. |
| Direction confirmation saves seat polarity. | Mirror confirmation records evidence only. | Avoid silently enabling unvalidated mirror recall. |
| Fixed release and settling pattern. | Bounded coarse-to-fine seat timing; latency and actual hold timing stay in private diagnostics. | Reduce visible stop/start gaps without adding technical controls to daily cards. |

The dark matte cards and two-column trial layout remain. Screenshots stay local/private.
